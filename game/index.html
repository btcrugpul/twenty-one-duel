TWENTY-ONE DUEL

Beat the dealer to 21. Bust and you lose the hand — closest to 21 takes the pot.

You

How to play: you and the dealer each get two cards. Number cards are face value, face cards count 10, an ace counts 11 or 1 — whichever helps you. Hit to draw another card, Stand to hold. The dealer draws until 17. Over 21 is a bust and loses at once. Win a hand to take the 10-chip bet; a tie pushes and your bet comes back. Every third win in a row pays a streak bonus of 10 more chips. Keys: D deal, H hit, S stand, N next hand. Run out of chips and the duel is over.
